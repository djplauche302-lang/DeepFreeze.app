How it works

Ten seconds at the sealer. Nothing after that.

A notebook taped to the lid works right up until you're standing in the store. Same list, except it's in your pocket — and it tells you what's getting old instead of waiting for you to notice.

At the sealer

Log it while your hands are already cold

Name, count, weight per pack, which freezer. Four taps while the sealer runs, and you never have to think about it again.

Months later

It tells you what to cook first

Every item ages against a real shelf life for what it is. When something has been in too long it turns red and climbs to the top of the list.

However many you have

Two freezers, or five

The kitchen one, the garage one, the chest out at the lake. Each one tracked on its own, switched with one tap. Set up as many as you need — the price is the same either way.
